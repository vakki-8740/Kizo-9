Terms & Conditions

Please read these points carefully before sending a complaint on the KIXO 9 GAME Help Center.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1 About This Help Center

  • This website is made only for taking complaints related to deposit, withdrawal and account problems, and for showing their status.
  • By sending a complaint here, you agree to these terms.
  • Sending a complaint is completely free. No one from our team will ever ask you to pay money to fix your complaint.

2 Give Correct Details

  • Enter your correct user name, registered mobile number, email ID and amount in the form.
  • Upload a clear and original screenshot. Edited or blurry screenshots slow down verification.
  • Wrong, incomplete or mismatched details can delay your complaint or lead to rejection.
  • Send only one complaint for one problem. Sending the same complaint again and again does not make it faster.

3 Deposit Complaints

  • Your balance is added only after your payment is verified in our records.
  • Your payment screenshot should clearly show the amount, date and time, and the success status.
  • If a payment has failed or is stuck at your bank or payment app, their refund and settlement time may also apply.

4 Withdrawal Complaints

  • Withdrawal requests are completed only after the account and request checks are passed.
  • Wrong bank or UPI details in your game account can cause your withdrawal to fail or get delayed.
  • Some delays can also happen from the bank or payment provider side, which are not in our control.

5 Account Safety

  • Send a complaint only for your own account. Never submit another person's details.
  • The game password in the form is used only by the support team to check your complaint.
  • Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, bank password or card CVV/PIN with anyone. Our team will never ask for these.
  • If any person asks you for money in our name to resolve a complaint, do not pay and inform us on Live Chat.

6 Status And Timing

  • Pending means your complaint is received and is being checked. Successful means it is resolved and completed.
  • You can see the status of all your complaints anytime on the All Complaints page.
  • We try to resolve most complaints within 30 minutes, but some cases need extra verification and can take more time.

7 Fake Complaints And Misuse

  • Complaints with fake screenshots, fake payments or false details will be rejected.
  • Repeated misuse of this page can lead to action on the game account as per the game rules.

8 Live Chat And Other Points

  • The Live Chat button may open an outside app or website. The rules of that service will apply there, so always use the official link given on the Home Page.
  • Use this website only if online gaming is allowed for you as per your age and the laws of your area.
  • These terms can be updated at any time. The new terms will apply from the time they are shown on this page.

By clicking Send Complain on any complaint form, you confirm that the details given by you are correct to the best of your knowledge.
